Privacy Policy for RAF Tracking

Application Bundle: com.houbara.hbtrack • Internal Wildlife Monitoring

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

1. Introduction & App Purpose

This Privacy Policy describes how the RAF Tracking application ("the App", "we", "our") handles account access, device capabilities, and data security. The App is a professional, internal organizational tool developed exclusively for authorized ecological researchers and conservation field personnel to monitor Houbara Bustard wildlife movement and execute field navigation operations.

2. Account Access & Provisioning

  • Internal Team Access Only: The App is designed solely for authorized members of the ecological research and field tracking operations team.
  • No Public Registration: The App does not offer public user self-registration. General public users cannot create accounts or submit personal information.
  • Administrator Provisioning: All user accounts, credentials, role tiers, and permissions are configured directly by organization Administrators and Managers.

3. Device Permissions & Information We Collect

To provide essential wildlife tracking and field navigation capabilities, the App requests access to the following:

  • Precise Location (GPS): Accessed exclusively while the App is actively in use to display your real-time field position relative to Houbara transmitter locations and compute navigation bearings. Location data is never used for background tracking or advertising.
  • Zero Tracking Across Third Parties: The App does NOT track users across third-party apps or websites. No advertising frameworks, ad identifiers (IDFA), or third-party marketing SDKs are embedded.
  • No Personal Data Harvesting: The App does not access, collect, or store your contacts, camera, photo library, microphone, financial records, or browsing history.

4. How We Use Information

Information accessed by the App is used strictly for ecological conservation and navigation:

  • To display real-time researcher positions on the interactive map relative to tagged wildlife devices.
  • To perform field distance calculations, bearing measurements, and historical trajectory inspections.
  • To ensure secure, authorized system access to field telemetry data.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

All telemetry and account records are managed through secure Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure with enterprise-grade encryption in transit (TLS 1.3/HTTPS) and at rest (AES-256):

  • Direct Backend Connection: The iOS application communicates directly with Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication using official native SDKs.
  • No Third-Party Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any user data or wildlife telemetry data to commercial third parties. All data remains strictly internal to the conservation organization.

6. Account Management & Deletion Requests

Because accounts are internally provisioned by organization leadership, users have the right to request deletion of their account credentials and associated user data at any time:

In-App Deletion Request: In the iOS app, navigate to Settings → Security → Request Account Deletion to send an automated deletion request to the administration team.

Alternatively, users or reviewers may submit an account deletion request directly by email to the administrator contact below. Account deletion requests are processed by Administrators within 48 hours.
